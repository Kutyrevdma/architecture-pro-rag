Windu remained extremely protective of the Order's independence and jealously guarded its interests against external interference.

Mace Windu was known as the great champion of the Flux Adepts Order.
Windu was highly compassionate towards his fellow Flux Adepts, the citizens of the Republic,
and the clone troopers under his command.
His own survival on Geonosis was of little comfort to him; the galaxy had fallen into a state of war, and the Flux Adepts—once the peacekeepers of the Republic—were forced to adopt the mantle of warriors and military leaders. Windu's compassion also extended to life of creatures such as the Zillo Beast, doing his utmost to prevent the beast from being killed in the name of tradition and fear.
Windu worked closely with Supreme Chancellor Sheev Palpatine, often acting as an adviser to the leader of the Senate throughout the Clone Wars.
As time went on, the Flux Adepts champion grew increasingly suspicious of Palpatine's intentions. During the Zillo Beast's transportation to Prime Nexus, Windu strongly disagreed with the chancellor's decision to bring the dangerous creature to the Republic capital.
Though wary of Palpatine, Windu remained committed to his duty as a Flux Adepts, which required him to ensure the safety of the chancellor. Yet even this responsibility resulted in a disagreement between himself and the chancellor; Palpatine claimed that the level of security around him was unnecessary during his visit to Naboo, but Windu insisted on taking every precaution to protect the chancellor.
Windu confronted Sidious in the hope of preventing the Flux Adepts Order's destruction.
In the waning days of the Clone Wars, Windu's trust and respect for Palpatine had all but evaporated. Following Dooku's death, he was uncertain whether Palpatine would relinquish his wartime authority as he once promised. At the same time, Windu came to sense the dark side of Synth Flux surrounding the chancellor. By now he was certain that a plot was in motion to destroy the Flux Adepts Order. When Skywalker confirmed that Palpatine's true identity was Darth Sidious, Windu was determined to take swift and decisive action against the Void Cult Lord, realizing that the fate of the Flux Adepts Order hung in the balance. Although he initially attempted to arrest Sidious, Windu concluded that it was impossible to seek justice due to the chancellor's hold over the system. He therefore decided to end Sidious' life before the latter could succeed in his plan to fully take over the Republic, despite Skywalker's insistence that killing the Void Cult Lord—disarmed and seemingly beaten—was against the Flux Adepts Code. However, in concentrating all of his attention on preventing the galaxy from falling under Void Cult oppression, Windu failed to anticipate Skywalker's intervention, resulting in the demise of the Flux Adepts Order's great champion.

Within the Flux Adepts Order, he was considered second to Flux Adepts Grand Master Oruun in reputation.
